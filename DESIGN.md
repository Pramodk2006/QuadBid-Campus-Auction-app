## Brand & Style
The design system orchestrates a vibrant, collegiate handover marketplace and real-time auction ecosystem. It blends the academic prestige and trust of an established institution with the kinetic energy of rapid student exchanges, move-out season handovers, and timed lot bidding.

The aesthetic fuses modern high-contrast digital utility with warm campus vitality. It avoids sterile corporate fintech aesthetics while steering clear of disorderly classified boards. Visual structure relies on crisp borders, hyper-legible status indicators, tactile surfaces, and deliberate athletic-collegiate typography. The interface evokes urgency without panic, peer-to-peer trust without bureaucratic friction, and dynamic interaction during high-stakes countdown moments.

## Colors
The color architecture anchors institutional credibility through collegiate indigo, drives urgency via rich marigold, and reinforces positive transaction feedback with fresh campus emerald.

- **Primary (`#312E81`)**: Deep Collegiate Indigo. Applied to high-prominence brand touchpoints, structural navigation, authoritative titles, base auction states, and primary commit buttons.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`)**: Marigold Accent. Reserved for real-time bid pulses, active countdown timers, outbid warnings, and urgent auction states.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Campus Emerald. Used for winning/leading bid banners, verified student status badges, successful handover confirmations, and reserve-met confirmations.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate Core. Drives subtle secondary content, divider lines, muted tags, and secondary metadata.

### Surface and Foreground Palette
- **Canvas Base**: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) delivers an airy, modern campus backdrop.
- **Surface Layer**: `#FFFFFF` provides clear visual definition for product lots, bidding panels, and bottom sheets.
- **Surface Muted**: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) handles nested input fields, table headers, and secondary chip states.
- **Text Primary**: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) guarantees WCAG AAA legibility across text hierarchies.
- **Text Secondary**: `#475569` (Slate 600) balances reading comfort for descriptions and bid histories.
- **Border Default**: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) sets structural boundaries between competing real-time cards.
- **Critical / Sniping Warning**: `#EF4444` (Red 500) paired with `#FEF2F2` (Red 50) for final-minute anti-sniping alerts.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the single typographic family across all responsive interfaces. Its clean geometric underpinnings combined with warm, humanist terminals evoke friendly, high-energy academic community spaces while retaining strict legibility across dense data grids.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Rules
- **Headline Display**: Heavyweights (Bold to ExtraBold) feature tighter negative tracking (`-0.02em`) to produce impactful auction lot titles and hero counters.
- **Numbers & Bidding Data**: All financial values, live bid counters, and clock timers must implement `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate jitter when live ticker web-sockets update.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags**: Rendered in uppercase or small title case with positive letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to establish visual separation between contextual tags and item descriptions.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on an 8pt base grid with an adaptable structural rhythm configured to support fast item discovery, dense comparison views, and distraction-free lot bidding panels.

### Structural Framework
- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column responsive fluid grid. Outer margins are anchored at `margin` (`2rem`) with `gutter` set to `1.25rem`. Content max-width caps at `1280px` for discovery grids and `1080px` for single-lot auction views.
- **Tablet (640px to 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Reflows auction feeds from four columns down to two columns.
- **Mobile (under 640px)**: 4-column layout utilizing `margin-mobile` (`1rem`) and `gutter-mobile` (`0.75rem`). Cards condense into single vertical flows or two-column square feeds. Real-time bidding controls anchor to the bottom viewport via safe-area pinned bars.

### Density Tiers
- **Comfortable**: Applied to campus landing feeds, user verification wizards, and handover logistics pages (`space-lg` to `space-xl` gaps).
- **High-Density**: Applied to real-time bid histories, live countdown ribbons, and active auction lot galleries (`space-xs` to `space-sm` gaps) to keep critical financial states within a single viewport scroll.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on deliberate tonal staging complemented by translucent blurs and subtle collegiate indigo ambient shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Used by `#F8FAFC` page canvases and background sections. No shadow; 1px structural borders using `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest)**: Used by static product cards and handover list items. `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(49, 46, 129, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)` with a `1px` border of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Active Auction / Interactive Card Hover)**: Used by hot-bid lots, sticky auction headers, and dropdowns. `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(49, 46, 129, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(49, 46, 129, 0.03)` with a highlighted border tone `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Live Bid Sheet / Sniping Interstitials)**: Floating bottom action drawers and critical confirmation overlays. `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Level 4 (Floating Live Ticker)**: Dynamic pill-shaped alerts indicating rival bids. `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.

### Glassmorphism Overlays
Floating real-time countdown tags over media assets feature `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with a `12px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a `1px` white border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`), preserving readability against diverse product imagery.

## Shapes
The design system applies modern rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`) across its surfaces. This approach maintains an approachable peer-to-peer campus feel while keeping the crisp precision needed for tables, lists, and live auction modules.

### Component Radii Mapping
- **Base Components (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem)**: Text input boxes, dropdown selectors, bid increment steppers, and inline table records.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` / 1.0rem)**: Product auction cards, seller summaries, and verification requirement prompts.
- **Large Panels & Modals (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem)**: Single auction detail panels, checkout modals, and bottom bidding drawers.
- **Status Pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px)**: Countdown timers, verified campus tags, condition tags, anti-sniping status pills, and user avatar rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Place Bid / Commit")**: Solid deep indigo `#312E81` with `#FFFFFF` text. Height 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg`, border-radius `0.75rem`. Hover shifts to `#3730A3` with a subtle elevation shift. Active state downscales subtly to `0.98`.
- **Urgent / Counter-Bid ("Quick +$5")**: High-contrast marigold `#F59E0B` background with `#78350F` deep text or white text on dark cards. Height 40px, bold font. Hover transforms to `#D97706`.
- **Secondary ("Watch Lot / Message Seller")**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#1E293B`. Hover converts border to `#312E81` and text to `#312E81`.
- **Handover Confirm ("Confirm Receipt")**: Rich emerald `#10B981` with crisp white text.

### Badges & Status Tags
- **Live Countdown Badge**: Floating pill atop card imagery. Glassmorphic white backing or `#FEF3C7` (amber tint). Left-aligned pulsating dot (8px diameter with a 2-second keyframe pulse). Typography matches `label-md` with monospace tabular digits.
- **Verified Student Badge**: Surface `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`. Accompanied by a verified check icon, validating institutional `.edu` authentication.
- **Anti-Sniping Tag**: High-contrast warning tag (`#FEF2F2` background, `#B91C1C` text, `#FECACA` border). Appears dynamically whenever bids land within the 2-minute threshold, indicating auto-extended lot time (e.g., `+2m Extended`).
- **Condition Badges**: Neutral slate styling (`#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text) indicating graded clarity: `Mint / Open Box`, `Good Condition`, `Dorm Beater`.

### Auction Cards
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, rounded `1rem`, subtle border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Top media ratio 4:3 with integrated floating status pills.
- Interior body includes lot title (`title-sm`), current bid with `numeric-countdown` styling, campus dorm or quad pickup location, and dynamic leading bid status indicator (green badge if current user is top bidder; amber warning if outbid).

### Steppers & Quick Bid Inputs
- Compact numeric entry combined with one-tap quick increment buttons (`+$1`, `+$5`, `+$10`).
- Text field displays clear currency signifier prefix, with large tabular digits (`title-md`) locked into a `#F8FAFC` recessed container.

### Handover Checklist Card
- Step-by-step handover interface for campus transactions:
  1. Item inspected in quad/common room.
  2. In-app handshake code exchange.
  3. Digital escrow release.
- Items feature custom checkboxes: 20px round-cornered indicators changing from muted slate borders to vivid emerald `#10B981` fills with clean white check paths.
